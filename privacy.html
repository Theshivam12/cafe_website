Privacy & data

Privacy Notice.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

1. What this website collects

This demo website does not intentionally store or transmit personal information through the contact form. The form asks for your name, email address and message only so that a future production implementation can respond to an enquiry.

2. Purpose limitation

If a production backend is connected, personal data should be collected only for specified purposes communicated to you at or before collection, such as responding to an enquiry, and should not be reused for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

3. Consent and notice

Where consent is the applicable basis for processing, the production website should provide a clear, standalone notice describing the personal data collected and the purpose of processing, with an easy mechanism to withdraw consent. The DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 contain requirements relevant to these practices.

4. Data minimisation & retention

A production implementation should collect only data that is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose and should define a retention period. Data should be deleted when the applicable purpose is no longer being served, subject to legal requirements.

5. Security

If a backend is added, reasonable security safeguards should be implemented, including HTTPS/TLS, server-side validation, authentication controls where needed, secure password hashing, parameterised database queries, access controls, CSRF protection where applicable, rate limiting and secure error handling.

6. Your rights

Depending on the applicable provisions and commencement timeline, individuals may have rights relating to access to information about personal data, correction/update, erasure, grievance redressal and withdrawal of consent. A production site should publish a practical method for exercising applicable rights.

7. Third-party services

This demo uses Google Fonts. A production deployment should review third-party services, cookies, analytics, maps, payment tools and other processors before enabling them, and update the privacy notice accordingly.

8. Contact

For this demo: hello@cafeshivam.example. Replace this with the café's real privacy contact before launch.

Important

This page is a website implementation template, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. The final privacy notice and operational data practices should be reviewed against the laws and rules applicable to the actual business, processing activities and commencement dates.
