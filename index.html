About Café Shivam

Where French charm meets Indian warmth.

Café Shivam is imagined as a neighbourhood café with a French soul — a place for buttery pastries, comforting plates and beautifully brewed coffee.

Come for a quick espresso, stay for a long conversation. Every detail is designed to feel welcoming without feeling ordinary.
